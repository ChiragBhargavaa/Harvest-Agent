# LeadReach Lead Analyst

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Reviews a business's website and scores how well it fits a brand's offer, returning up to three evidence-backed outreach hooks (each with the exact snippet and URL).

**Message:** `ToolRequest(tool="qualify_leads", args_json='{...}')` → `ToolResponse(ok, result, error)`. Part of the LeadReach multi-agent system (orchestrator: `leadreach`).

**Keywords:** lead qualification, website review, fit score, sales intelligence
