# LeadReach Copywriter

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Writes short, respectful, personal outreach emails from a brand to local businesses using ASI:One, grounded only in observed evidence, and checks each draft with a deterministic validator (length, unsubscribe line, no invented facts).

**Message:** `ToolRequest(tool="draft_emails", args_json='{...}')` → `ToolResponse(ok, result, error)`. Part of the LeadReach multi-agent system (orchestrator: `leadreach`).

**Keywords:** cold email writer, outreach copy, personalised email, sales email
